DECIMAG · Right-to-Act

Authorization is not the same as the right to act

DECIMAG reconstructs a decision-relevant computational representation of organizational reality, then evaluates proposed actions against that reality before execution.

DECIMAG evaluates proposed actions from AI agents and enterprise systems against the organization’s represented structural reality before execution.

The missing decision

Access answers one question.
Execution requires another.

Identity, access and policy systems establish who may reach a system and what permissions they hold.

But a permitted action can still conflict with the organization as it actually exists now: its structure, responsibilities, dependencies and current conditions.

DECIMAG evaluates whether the proposed action has the right to proceed in that organizational reality.
