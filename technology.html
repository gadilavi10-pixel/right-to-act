Technology

Structure before execution

DECIMAG reconstructs a decision-relevant computational representation of organizational structure and evaluates proposed actions against it.

The structural path

Sources → Structure Acquisition → Organizational Reality

Sources

Organizational evidence

Relevant organizational sources provide evidence about structure, responsibilities, relationships and current conditions.

Structure Acquisition

Computational reconstruction

DECIMAG builds the decision-relevant representation used at evaluation time.

Organizational Reality

Versioned decision context

The resulting representation captures the organizational reality relevant to a proposed action.

This describes the role of Structure Acquisition, not its proprietary implementation.

Structural evaluation

The action is evaluated against reality

Organizational Reality is an input to Structural Evaluation. It is not a linear gate that the action passes through.

The evaluation produces a Right-to-Act outcome: ALLOW, DENY, REQUEST_INFO, DEFER or ESCALATE.

Memory and versioning support review of what organizational reality informed a decision.
