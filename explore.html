FAQ

Direct answers

What is DECIMAG?

The DECIMAG runtime represents the organization as a computational structure. Before execution, it evaluates each Proposed Action against the current Organizational Reality.

Is DECIMAG a cybersecurity product?

No. Security signals may provide context for a DECIMAG decision, and security systems may receive its outcome. Cybersecurity does not define the product.

Is DECIMAG a policy engine?

No. Policy can provide context, but DECIMAG is not a policy engine or a policy extraction system.

Does Decision Relay make decisions?

No. Decision Relay transports a decision after the Right-to-Act outcome already exists.

Is the console connected to live customer data?

No. The public product demonstration uses fixture data. It preserves explicit seams for future Runtime integration.

How does Structure Acquisition work?

The site explains the architectural role of Structure Acquisition. It constructs the structural representation used by the runtime. Proprietary implementation details are not disclosed.
