FAQ

Direct answers

What is DECIMAG?

DECIMAG is a working technology that reconstructs a decision-relevant computational representation of organizational reality and evaluates proposed actions against it before execution.

Is DECIMAG a cybersecurity product?

No. Security signals and systems may be inputs, recipients or consequences of a DECIMAG decision, but cybersecurity does not define the product.

Is DECIMAG a policy engine?

No. Policy can contribute context, but DECIMAG is not defined as a policy engine or policy extraction system.

Does Decision Relay make decisions?

No. Decision Relay transports a decision that has already been made.

Is the console connected to live customer data?

No. The public product demonstration uses fixture data and preserves explicit seams for future Runtime integration.

How does Structure Acquisition work?

The site explains its architectural role: reconstructing a decision-relevant representation of organizational reality. Proprietary implementation details are not disclosed.
